## **Q: Do you edit photos to match a specific style or aesthetic?**

My editing style is designed to enhance the natural beauty of each moment while staying true to the authentic colors and emotions of your day.

I don't impose a heavy filter or preset look — instead, I work with the natural elements like location, lighting, outfits, and overall atmosphere of the day.

For example, if you're wearing gothic outfits in a candle-lit church, the photos will naturally carry a darker, more cinematic tone — that's part of the beauty of honest storytelling.

If you have a specific vision or aesthetic in mind, we can plan ahead together to make sure everything works in harmony to achieve that look.

## **Q: How do you make sure your work reflects each couple's unique personality and story?**

That's exactly what my style is all about.

When I give couples gentle direction, I usually suggest simple prompts — like hugging, kissing, or looking into each other's eyes. Each couple expresses love in their own way, and their natural reactions after these moments are always different.

That's why every gallery feels unique — it's a reflection of your connection, not a repeated pose.

## **Q: How does the booking, contract, and payment process work?**

Once a couple reaches out, we'll first exchange some details — your wedding date, location(s), number of guests, coverage time, and what kind of photos or video you're looking for.

After confirming that I'm available and you'd like to move forward, I'll send over a contract for review. Once you've signed the contract and paid a 50% deposit, your date will be officially secured.

I accept check, Venmo, or Zelle for payments.

The remaining balance is due within one week after the wedding, and once the full payment is received, I'll send out your photo gallery.
