## **Q: Our Approach**

My work is rooted in a documentary style — I believe the most meaningful moments are the ones that unfold naturally, without force or staging.

Building on that foundation, I use my knowledge of composition, color, light, and post-production to create images with cinematic depth and emotional storytelling.

When couples aren't sure what to do in front of the camera, I like to offer gentle direction and small prompts—little actions or interactions that give you something to do and help you feel at ease.

My goal is for you to enjoy the experience, stay present in the moment, and have your story captured in the most genuine and beautiful way possible.

This approach applies to both my photo and video work.

## **Q: How do you plan your shooting process and manage the pace on the wedding day?**

Every wedding has its own unique flow and rhythm, so I don't follow a fixed template. Instead, I adjust the shooting sequence according to the day's schedule to make sure no time is wasted and the wedding runs smoothly.

I usually work with couples to create a personalized photo list to ensure that all the important people, moments, and details are captured. My goal is always to capture as many beautiful moments as possible while keeping the pace comfortable and aligned with each couple's needs.

✨ **A little tip:**

One of the trickiest parts of the day is often the family photo session — especially when there are many guests. If this part happens before the reception, it helps a lot to have a cocktail area where guests can relax while waiting.

I recommend coming up with a family photo list in advance and sharing it with the people involved, so everyone knows when they're needed for photos. This way, they can enjoy cocktail hour without waiting around unnecessarily.

It also helps to assign someone who knows most of the family members to manage the list and call people over when it's their turn — this keeps things smooth, organized, and efficient.

## **Q: Do you offer video or short film services? Are there any photo + video packages available?**

Yes, I do! My Premium Package is a customized photo package, while the Exclusive Package focuses on video coverage. Both packages can be booked separately or together for a complete photo + video experience.

## **Q: Do you take on out-of-town or destination weddings? How does the pricing work?**

Yes, absolutely! I'd love to travel with you and capture your special day in a new place.

For weddings outside of my home market, there will be an additional travel fee. The exact amount depends on the destination and travel distance.

## **Q: How long does it take to receive the final photos or video after the wedding?**

I'll upload all the photos within a week after the wedding for you to review.

You can then select the images you'd like to have edited, and the edited photos will be delivered within one month through an online gallery.

## **Q: Can the photo or video style be customized based on our preferences?**

Of course! I love collaborating with couples to create something that reflects your unique taste and story.

That said, the final look and feel of the photos or film can be influenced by the location, lighting, outfits, and overall atmosphere of the day.

For example, if you're wearing gothic outfits in a candle-lit church, the photos will naturally carry a darker, more cinematic tone — that's part of the beauty of honest storytelling.

If you have a specific vision or aesthetic in mind, we can plan ahead together to make sure everything works in harmony to achieve that look.

## **Q: How do you make sure your work reflects each couple's unique personality and story?**

That's exactly what my style is all about.

When I give couples gentle direction, I usually suggest simple prompts — like hugging, kissing, or looking into each other's eyes. Each couple expresses love in their own way, and their natural reactions after these moments are always different.

That's why every gallery feels unique — it's a reflection of your connection, not a repeated pose.

## **Q: How does the booking, contract, and payment process work?**

Once a couple reaches out, we'll first exchange some details — your wedding date, location(s), number of guests, coverage time, and what kind of photos or video you're looking for.

After confirming that I'm available and you'd like to move forward, I'll send over a contract for review. Once you've signed the contract and paid a 50% deposit, your date will be officially secured.

I accept check, Venmo, or Zelle for payments.

The remaining balance is due within one week after the wedding, and once the full payment is received, I'll send out your photo gallery.
